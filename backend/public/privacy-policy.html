1. Introduction

Atominos Consulting Private Limited ("we", "us", "our") operates the Nahom mobile application (available on Android and iOS) and associated backend services (collectively, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Service.

By creating an account or using Nahom, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the terms, please do not access or use the Service.

2. About Nahom

Nahom is a gated community management platform that enables residents, administrators, security guards, and facility staff to manage day-to-day community operations. Core features include:

  • Community directory and resident profiles
  • Visitor pass management with QR code verification
  • Maintenance billing, invoicing, and payment collection
  • Complaint and maintenance request management
  • Community notes and notice board
  • Amenity booking with QR-based check-in
  • Community polls
  • In-app messaging between residents
  • Real-time notifications
  • Role-based access control for different user types

3. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to all users of the Nahom mobile application (Android and iOS), the Nahom web administration portal, and any associated APIs and services. It covers all personal information processed in connection with your use of the Service, regardless of the device or platform you use.

4. Information You Provide

4.1 Account Registration

When you create a Nahom account, we collect:

  • Email address (required) — used as your primary login identifier
  • Username (required) — used as a unique display identifier
  • Password (required for non-SSO accounts) — stored as a bcrypt hash; we never store passwords in plain text
  • Full name (optional) — for display purposes within your community
  • Phone number (optional) — for OTP-based login, directory contact, and SMS notifications

4.2 Single Sign-On (SSO)

You may register or log in using Google or Microsoft SSO. When you use SSO, we receive your name, email address, and a unique provider identifier from the SSO provider. We do not receive or store your SSO provider password.

4.3 Profile Information

You may optionally provide:

  • Profile photo (avatar) — uploaded and stored on our servers
  • Interests — displayed in the community directory
  • Notification preferences — your email and SMS notification opt-in/opt-out choices
  • Directory privacy settings — whether to show your phone number, allow messages, allow intercom calls, or hide yourself from the community directory

4.4 Community and Unit Information

Upon joining a community, the following information is associated with your account through your community membership:

  • Organization/community membership and assigned role(s)
  • Villa/flat/unit number, block/building, floor
  • Residency type (e.g., Owner, Tenant)

5. Information Collected Automatically

5.1 Session and Device Information

When you log in, Nahom records session information for security and session management purposes:

  • IP address
  • Device name
  • Browser type
  • Operating system
  • Last activity timestamp

This information is stored in a session record that expires automatically and is used solely for authentication, security monitoring, and abuse prevention.

5.2 Audit Logs

Administrative actions within the platform are logged for security and accountability purposes. Audit logs contain the acting user's ID, the action performed, a target reference, metadata, and the user's IP address.

5.3 Security Logs

Amenity check-in/check-out events and QR scan activities are recorded in security logs, including the user involved, the guard who performed the scan, scan time, gate name, and scan type. These logs support community security operations.

5.4 Information We Do NOT Collect

Based on the current implementation, Nahom does not collect:

  • Precise or approximate GPS/device location
  • Contact lists or address books
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA, GAID)
  • Analytics or usage tracking data via third-party analytics SDKs
  • Crash reports via third-party crash reporting SDKs
  • Biometric data
  • Microphone or audio recordings
  • Background location data

6. Community and Profile Information

Nahom organizes users into communities (organizations). Each community has its own membership records, roles, units (villas/flats), and operational data. Your profile information is scoped to the communities you belong to.

Directory Privacy Controls: You have the ability to control your visibility in the community directory through the following settings:

  • Show or hide your phone number from other residents
  • Allow or disallow direct messages from other residents
  • Allow or disallow intercom calls
  • Hide your profile entirely from the community directory

7. User-Generated Content

7.1 Community Notes

You can post short community notes (up to 80 characters) visible to members of your community. Notes include a category, an optional emoji, and an expiration date after which they are automatically deactivated.

7.2 Notice Board Posts

Authorized users can publish notices to the community notice board, which may include text content and file attachments.

7.3 Community Polls

Authorized users can create polls within the community. Your poll votes are recorded and associated with your user account.

7.4 File Uploads

You may upload images and documents in the context of complaints, amenity bookings, notice board posts, payment proofs, and profile photos. Uploaded files are stored on our servers and served via authenticated access. The mobile application requests camera and photo library permissions solely for this purpose.

8. Maintenance and Complaint Information

When you submit a maintenance complaint, the following information is collected:

  • Category and sub-category of the complaint
  • Title and description
  • Priority level
  • Location details (building, floor, flat, common area, landmark)
  • Preferred visit date and time window
  • File attachments (photos/documents)
  • Your name, phone number, and unit information (auto-populated from your profile)

Complaint records include a full status history timeline, technician assignment details, resolution notes, and optional feedback ratings (1–5 stars for categories such as overall satisfaction, technician performance, cleanliness, and communication).

9. Messaging and Communication

9.1 In-App Messaging

Nahom provides a directory messaging feature that allows community members to send messages to each other. Messages include the message text, sender and receiver identifiers, timestamps, and delivery/read status. Messages are scoped to your community and stored on our servers.

Important: Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. They are transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS) and stored in our database, but they can be accessed by authorized system administrators for operational or compliance purposes.

9.2 Real-Time Notifications

Nahom uses WebSocket connections (Socket.io) to deliver real-time in-app notifications. Notifications include a title, body, type, and an optional action URL. They are stored in your notification inbox on our servers.

9.3 Email and SMS Communications

Nahom may send you transactional emails (e.g., OTP codes, invitation links, invoice notifications) and SMS messages (e.g., OTP codes for phone-based login). These communications are sent via third-party email and SMS providers as described in Section 15.

10. Payment and Transaction Information

10.1 What Nahom Stores

Nahom stores the following payment and financial data:

  • Payment amount, currency (default: INR), and payment date
  • Payment status (pending, processing, success, failed, refunded, etc.)
  • Payment category (online or offline)
  • Payment method description (e.g., "credit_card", "bank_transfer", "cash", "cheque")
  • Gateway transaction ID (a reference ID from the payment provider)
  • Payment reference numbers and receipt numbers
  • Proof documents for offline payments (uploaded files)
  • Invoice details: billing period, line items, subtotal, tax, outstanding balance, due date, payment history
  • Approval status and associated reviewer/approver information

10.2 Online Payments — Razorpay

For online payments, Nahom integrates with Razorpay, a third-party payment gateway. When you make an online payment:

  • You are redirected to Razorpay's secure payment interface to enter your card, bank, or UPI details
  • Nahom does not store, process, or have access to your credit/debit card numbers, bank account numbers, or UPI PINs. These are handled entirely by Razorpay
  • After payment completion, Nahom receives a transaction ID and payment status from Razorpay via webhook
  • Razorpay's privacy practices are governed by their own privacy policy: https://razorpay.com/privacy/

10.3 Offline Payments

Nahom supports recording offline payments (cash, cheque, bank transfer). For offline payments, authorized administrators may record the payment method, reference number, and upload a proof document (e.g., a photo of a cheque or bank transfer receipt). This information is stored on our servers.

10.4 Wallet

Nahom maintains an internal wallet and ledger system to track resident balances, credits, and debits within a community. Wallet records include transaction amounts, types, and associated invoice or payment references.

11. Visitor Management and Security

Nahom provides visitor pass management for gated communities. When creating a visitor pass, the following visitor information may be collected:

  • Visitor name and phone number
  • ID proof type and ID proof number (for identity-verified passes)
  • Vehicle details (vendor, number plate, vehicle type)
  • Delivery details (partner name, order ID, package count)
  • Service details (category, notes)
  • Visit purpose and validity period

Visitor pass data is associated with the resident who created the pass and the community. QR codes are generated for pass verification by security personnel.

Visitor Log: Entry and exit events for visitors are recorded for community security purposes, including timestamps and the security personnel who processed the visit.

14. Community Visibility and Role-Based Access

Nahom implements a dynamic role-based access control (RBAC) system. Each community can define custom roles with granular permissions. Typical roles and their data access include:

Role Type Data Access
Resident Own profile, own unit details, own complaints, own invoices and payments, own visitor passes, community directory (subject to other residents' privacy settings), community notes, notice board, polls, in-app messages with other residents
Community Administrator All resident profiles within the community, all unit details, all complaints, all invoices and payments, billing configuration, amenity management, role management, community settings, audit logs
Security / Guard Visitor passes for verification, QR scanning for amenity check-in, security logs, limited resident information (name, unit) needed for visitor verification
Facility / Technician Assigned complaints, work orders, and relevant resident contact information for service delivery
Super Administrator Platform-level access across all communities for system management and support

Directory Privacy: Residents can control their visibility in the community directory. If a resident hides from the directory, their profile will not be visible to other residents (though administrators retain access for management purposes).

16. Data Storage and Security

We implement the following security measures to protect your data:

16.1 Transport Security

  • All communication between the mobile application and our servers is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS encrypted connections
  • WebSocket connections for real-time features use encrypted transport

16.2 Authentication Security

  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt with automatic salting before storage; plain-text passwords are never stored
  • OTP codes are hashed before storage and automatically expire via TTL (time-to-live) database indexes
  • Refresh tokens are hashed before storage in session records
  • JWT access tokens are signed with a server-side secret key
  • Sensitive credentials are stored on the device using Expo SecureStore (encrypted device storage)

16.3 Application Security

  • Security headers enforced via Helmet middleware
  • Input sanitization against NoSQL injection (express-mongo-sanitize) and XSS attacks (xss-clean)
  • Input validation on all API endpoints using express-validator
  • Rate limiting on authentication and OTP endpoints to prevent brute-force attacks, backed by Redis
  • CORS restrictions to authorized origins
  • Role-based access control enforced at the API level with permission checks
  • Correlation ID tracking on every request for security traceability

16.4 File Storage

Uploaded files (profile photos, complaint attachments, payment proofs) are stored on the application server's file system and served via authenticated routes.

18. Account Deletion

Nahom provides a direct, self-service account deletion feature within the mobile application, adhering to Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v) and Google Play Store User Data Policy.

18.1 In-App Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and associated personal data at any time directly through the app:

  1. Open the Nahom mobile application.
  2. Navigate to Settings (accessible from your profile or bottom navigation).
  3. Scroll to the Account section.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Review the confirmation dialog and tap Delete Permanently.

18.2 What Happens Upon Deletion

When you delete your account, Nahom implements the following automated data scrubbing process:

  • Personal Data Anonymization: Your full name is reset to an anonymous placeholder, your phone number and profile avatar are permanently cleared, and your login email is unlinked and replaced with a randomized tombstone identifier.
  • Community Disassociation: You are removed from all community villa/flat resident lists, and all active community memberships are deleted.
  • User Content Purging: All community notes you created are permanently removed.
  • Messages Deleted: All direct messages you sent or received and associated conversation threads are permanently deleted.
  • Notifications Cleared: All notification records addressed to you are permanently deleted.
  • Complaint Data Anonymized: Any personal contact details (name, email, phone) stored within your maintenance complaint records are anonymized. The complaint operational records are retained for community facility history.
  • SSO Identities Removed: All linked Single Sign-On identities (Google, Microsoft) are permanently deleted.
  • Immediate Session Revocation: All active login sessions, JWT tokens, and device credentials are fully revoked, preventing any subsequent authentication.
  • Uploaded Files Cleaned: Your profile avatar file is removed from the server. Complaint attachment files are retained for community operational records.
  • Financial & Regulatory Retention: Historical financial records (tax invoices, payment ledger entries, and receipts) and security/audit logs are retained in an anonymized form to comply with applicable tax, corporate accounting, and community governance laws.

18.3 Alternative Deletion Request

If you are unable to access the mobile application, you may submit an account deletion request by emailing info@atominosconsulting.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" from your registered email address. Verified deletion requests will be processed within 30 business days.
