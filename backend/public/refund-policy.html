Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: October 16, 2026  |  Last Updated: October 16, 2026

1. Scope

This policy covers payments made in Nahom: maintenance and billing invoices, amenity bookings and wallet top-ups. Payments are processed by Razorpay and are for services provided by your community, not by Nahom.

2. Wallet top-ups

  • Unused wallet balance can be refunded to the original UPI/card/bank account used for the top-up, using the refund option in the Wallet screen.
  • Amounts already used for bookings or dues are not refundable from the wallet, but may be refunded under section 3.

3. Amenity bookings and cancellations

  • Each amenity shows its own cancellation window and refund rules before you confirm a booking.
  • Cancel within the allowed window and the eligible amount is returned to the wallet or original payment method.
  • If the community cancels or blocks a booking (for example maintenance), you receive a full refund.

4. Maintenance and billing invoices

Invoices are issued by your community. If you believe you were charged incorrectly, raise it with your community administrator. Verified overpayments and duplicate payments are refunded.

5. Failed or duplicate payments

If money was debited but the payment failed or was charged twice, it is automatically reversed by the payment partner, usually within 5–7 working days.

6. How refunds are paid

Refunds go to the original payment method. Banks may take 5–7 working days to show the credit.

7. Need help?

Write to us with your payment reference and we will help resolve it.
